Logo and name ideas

The project grew from a quick-cut phone app into a multi-lane editor (NLE). The name Pocket Cut now sells the smaller idea. Each file here is both a logo and a candidate name; the colors are free to differ from the app's yellow, because the app's accent can follow the logo.

How I scored (1 to 5 each, 25 max): Distinct = does it look unlike other apps? Small-size = readable at 20 to 48 px (the home screen is where it lives). Scope fit = says "robust editor", not "quick trimmer". Name = short, speakable, pleasant. Ownable = can you realistically get the name, handle and search results? These are my judgments from the drawings and general knowledge; I have not searched trademarks or app stores, so check any favorite before committing.

My pick: Playhead for coherence (it is the app's own signature gesture, and it keeps the current yellow), with Offcut as the more playful, more ownable alternative. If you want a total change of mood, Cutroom.

My overall judgment (so you only need this page)

Where I land: only Playhead scores clearly above the rest (22 of 25), and the gap to second place is four points, which is large on this scale. The reason is not that it is the prettiest: it is the only one whose idea comes from how this app really feels to use (the fixed yellow line you slide the timeline under), and it needs no new accent color. Everything else is a clever picture of a general video idea.

Honest weaknesses of my favorite: the drawing is plain. In the render the line plus triangle head can read as a pin or a golf tee, and three gray bars are a very standard timeline picture. It needs a more unusual silhouette (for example a head shaped like a notch cut from the strip, or the line breaking one strip in two) before it is ownable. The name "Playhead" is also a plain editing term, so expect to need a suffix or a different domain.

Second tier: Offcut is the most original name and mark, but it sells trimming, not the full editor. Frameline is the safest corporate choice. Cutroom has the strongest personality if you want warmth over precision.

Rule out: Splice (an existing well-known editor), Notch (points at phone hardware and is an existing tool name), Stack (too generic everywhere).

Limits of this review: scores are one person's judgment from drawings and general knowledge, the criteria are equally weighted although you may care more about some (for example Ownable), I did not check trademarks, domains or app stores, and nothing was tested with real users. Treat it as a way to argue, not as a result.
